## 1. Transparency & Verification Policy

PlantAI prioritizes scientific transparency and strict safety conservatism:
1. **No Inventions:** We never fabricate clinical citations or synthesize unverifiable references. Claims without direct open-access pharmacopoeia backing remain marked internally with `TODO(verify)`.
2. **Soft, Non-Curative Language:** All traditional and folk uses are strictly phrased as *'traditionally used for...'* or *'reported in folk use'*. The app makes **zero** curative assertions (`treats`, `cures`, `proven`) and provides **no** medicinal dosages.
3. **Safety & Toxicity Precedence:** Potential hazards (e.g., pediatric neem oil toxicity, bitter bottle gourd cucurbitacins, moringa root bark spirochin, oleander cardenolides) remain prominently highlighted under cautious toxicity warnings.
4. **Public Interface Separation:** The raw developer marker `TODO(verify)` is scrubbed from customer-facing web views. Instead, unverified species carry a prominent disclaimer: *'Educational information, not medical advice. Some details are not independently verified.'*
